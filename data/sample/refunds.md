# Refund disputes

Source ID: refund-policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-06
Status: current
Authority: DisputeLens fictional payment-provider policy
Scope: refunds linked to payments taken by small merchants

This policy is fictional and used for the DisputeLens evaluation.

## Customer says a refund did not arrive

Locate the original payment and check whether a refund was promised, initiated, completed, failed or cancelled. Record the refund amount, currency, date, original transaction reference and any refund reference shown by the payment provider.

Keep the customer message confirming the refund agreement and any provider record of its status. A promise to refund is not the same as a completed refund. Do not tell the customer that money has been returned solely because a staff member agreed to process it.

## Partial or multiple refunds

Compare the total refunded amount with the amount agreed. Identify each refund separately and explain any outstanding balance. Do not present a partial refund as a full refund.

## Pending or failed refunds

If the provider shows a refund as pending or failed, state that status and request clarification from the provider. This policy does not specify how many days it takes a refund to appear on a customer's statement.

## Missing records and boundaries

If no refund reference or status record is available, say which information needs checking. Do not invent a reference, completion date or arrival estimate. This policy sets no dispute-response deadline and does not guarantee an outcome. Do not initiate another refund solely from the assistant's answer.
