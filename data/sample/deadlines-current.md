# Chargeback response deadline — current general guidance

Source ID: deadline-general-current
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2026-10-06
Status: current
Authority: DisputeLens fictional payment-provider guidance
Scope: merchant responses to fictional provider chargeback notifications

This policy is fictional. Its deadlines are test data, not real card-scheme or payment-provider rules.

## Notification with an explicit deadline

Use the response deadline stated on the applicable chargeback notification. Check its date, time and timezone. A date without a time or timezone needs clarification from the provider; do not supply those details yourself.

## Notification without an explicit deadline

This guidance says the merchant has **seven calendar days** from the notification date to respond when the notification gives no explicit deadline. It does not explain whether the notification date counts as day one or specify a cutoff time. Ask the provider before calculating an exact cutoff.

## Applicability and contradictions

This is current general guidance. It does not establish that it overrides other current guidance from the same provider. If another applicable current source gives a different rule, show both and seek clarification rather than selecting a deadline silently.

## Boundaries

Merchant response deadlines are not interchangeable with cardholder filing limits or scheme-level time limits. This policy does not define those limits. A previously superseded version must not be used as the current rule.
