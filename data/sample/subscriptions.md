# Subscription disputes

Source ID: subscription-policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-06
Status: current
Authority: DisputeLens fictional payment-provider policy
Scope: recurring subscriptions sold by small merchants

This policy is fictional and used for the DisputeLens evaluation.

## Customer disputes a recurring payment

Check the subscription agreement, billing frequency, amount and renewal date. Locate the record showing which terms the customer accepted and when. A successful payment alone does not establish that the customer agreed to recurring billing.

## Customer says they cancelled

Find the cancellation request, the time it was received and any cancellation confirmation. Compare these records with the disputed payment's billing period and the cancellation terms accepted by the customer.

Under this fictional policy, a cancellation received before the next renewal prevents that renewal charge. A cancellation received after renewal prevents future renewal charges; whether the current period should be refunded depends on the applicable agreed refund terms. Do not infer the customer's refund entitlement from the cancellation date alone.

## Unclear dates or missing terms

If the renewal and cancellation order cannot be established, identify the missing timestamps and ask for clarification. If the accepted cancellation terms are unavailable, do not assume a notice period or create one.

## Boundaries

This policy does not cover hotel no-shows, one-off purchases or service-quality disputes. It does not set a chargeback-response deadline, authorise continued billing after cancellation or guarantee a dispute outcome.
