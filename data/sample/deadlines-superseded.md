## Historical rule

This former guidance allowed **fourteen calendar days** from the notification date when no response deadline was stated on the notification. It has been replaced by version 2.0 of the current general guidance.

## How to use this document

Exclude this source from ordinary retrieval for current merchant questions. Do not combine its fourteen-day rule with current guidance or present it as an alternative current deadline.

An explicitly historical question would require checking which policy applied at that time. This prototype does not infer historical applicability from a transaction date alone.
