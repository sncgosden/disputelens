# Delivery disputes

Source ID: delivery-policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-10-06
Status: current
Authority: DisputeLens fictional payment-provider policy
Scope: physical goods sold by small merchants and shipped to customers

This policy is fictional and used for the DisputeLens evaluation.

## Customer says an order did not arrive

Check the order reference, agreed delivery address, promised delivery date and items purchased. Compare these with the carrier's tracking record and any available delivery confirmation. Keep relevant messages about delays, address changes or the customer's complaint.

A tracking status of “delivered” is a useful record, but this policy does not treat that status alone as conclusive proof that the customer received the correct order at the agreed address. Check what the delivery confirmation actually shows.

## Partial delivery

If only some items arrived, identify the missing items. Keep shipment records for each parcel and any record of a replacement or refund. Do not describe a partially delivered order as fully delivered.

## Missing or contradictory records

If delivery confirmation is unavailable, explain which records are missing. Do not invent a signature, photograph or delivery time. If tracking and customer messages conflict, retain both and request clarification from the carrier or payment provider.

## Boundaries

This policy covers shipped physical goods. It does not establish requirements for digital products, hotel bookings or completed services. It sets no response deadline and does not guarantee that a dispute will be resolved in the merchant's favour.
