This policy is fictional. Its deadline deliberately conflicts with the current general guidance to test the assistant's behaviour.

## Notification with an explicit deadline

Use the deadline stated on the applicable notification. Check its date, time and timezone. Ask the provider if the notification is incomplete or contradictory.

## Notification without an explicit deadline

This support guidance says the merchant has **five business days** from the notification date to respond when the notification gives no explicit deadline. It does not define holidays, cutoff time or how to count the notification date. Do not calculate an exact deadline without those details.

## Relationship to other current guidance

The support guidance has the same stated authority as the current general guidance. Neither source contains a precedence rule. The general guidance instead states seven calendar days for the same situation. Neither the word “support” nor the version number establishes which source wins.

## What to do with this conflict

Cite both applicable sources, explain their different units and durations, and ask the provider to confirm the deadline. Do not blend them, choose the longer period or present one as authoritative without further evidence.
